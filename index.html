04 / On AI

How AI fits into my work.

I use AI to move faster, not to hand off responsibility. Here’s the approach I bring to it.

How I see it

AI can accelerate work I understand; it cannot replace understanding it. I’m accountable for anything I build, so I stay close to the fundamentals and verify the result.

Tools I reach for

I use ChatGPT and Claude to think through problems, GitHub Copilot for inline suggestions, and Kiro when I’m navigating a codebase quickly. Each is useful in a different moment.

What I use it for

Debugging, learning unfamiliar concepts, handling boilerplate, and rubber-ducking my reasoning. Explaining a problem out loud often reveals the assumption I missed.

Where I’m careful

Especially in embedded and low-level work. Confident-sounding output can still be subtly wrong, so I rely on my own testing, datasheets, and hardware-level reasoning.

The other side of engineering.

Autonomy · Design Justice

The Ethics of Autonomous Vehicles

Using autonomous vehicles as a case study, I examine who is left out of the design process and how centering affected communities could lead to better decisions.

AI · Human Care

Should AI Care Robots Pretend to Care?

Care robots can address real needs, but many are designed to feel like companions. I argue that, particularly in elder and child care, they should remain clearly robotic rather than imitate a relationship.
